## Required rule metadata before production

Authority, jurisdiction, source URL, document identifier, clause/page, effective interval, applicability, superseding version, last-reviewed date and verification status. Keep policy wording and regulatory authority separate.

## Verification work still open

Acquire official circular and handbook documents through an accessible official route or user-provided copies. Verify intervening amendments through 24 October 2026, current grievance prerequisites, policy wording and settlement-document examples. Statistical context must never become an individual coverage conclusion.
