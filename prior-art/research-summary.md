# ClaimMe research findings

Reviewed: 2026-10-24. This is design research, not a validated regulatory rule registry or a production-readiness claim.

## Product implications

1. A status needs a reason, source, actor, and next action. Repeated review stages are different events, not backward progress.
2. Document receipt, readability, extraction, verification, and insurer acceptance are separate states. A successful upload proves none of the later states.
3. A document request should identify the requested evidence and the original communication. Retain previous uploads and requests.
4. Preserve original insurer wording alongside the plain-language explanation. Explain uncertainty explicitly.
5. Treat a settlement decision and actual payment as separate events. Reconcile line items against the letter before explaining deductions.
6. Grievance preparation requires prior representations, replies, supporting evidence, and a jurisdiction-specific eligibility check.

## Research limits

Public help pages were read; authenticated portals were not audited. No claim submission, customer-data collection, or insurer integration occurred. Source workflow descriptions are not proof of their live UI quality. No claim-settlement statistics have been incorporated.

The IRDAI health FAQ, circular library, and handbook direct pages returned HTTP 403. Search listings identified the 2024-25 handbook and May 2024 circular, but their full authoritative documents and subsequent amendment history remain unverified. Do not publish regulatory deadlines or market figures until that verification is complete.
